02 / 10 · Blockers

Five gaps prevent a faithful product GLB.

Repeated fixes to individual images improve examples, but do not establish generalization.

01 · Recognition

Thin rims, clear lenses, shadows and occlusion cause missed or conflicting parts.

02 · Geometry

Masks do not determine thickness, curvature, hidden hinges or correct component joints.

03 · Appearance

Photo lighting gets mixed with colour, lens tint, transparency and surface finish.

04 · Evidence

No independent unseen-product benchmark or reference geometry proves the target.

05 · Capacity

Reported Python usage is roughly 4 GB per run; shared admission and memory measurements are needed.

Why another SAM swap is insufficient

Better masks address visible evidence. Geometry, materials and validation remain separate work.

08 / 10 · Data strategy

A catalog and a training dataset serve different purposes.

Fittingbox has a large ready-made frame database. Its precise AI training data is not disclosed in the reviewed pages.

Required

Evaluation set

Required to demonstrate quality: diverse unseen products, independent part labels and a trusted geometry subset. Include failures.

Conditional

Custom training data

Required if we train/fine-tune specialized models. Labels and volume depend on the task; no justified universal count. Pretrained models already embody prior training.

Recommended for proposed build

Adjustable geometry library

Frame families, arm profiles and hardware can help constrain fitting. Similar templates are not exact product matches.

Optional asset source

Worldwide frame catalog

Optional for reconstruction. Exact SKU/colour matching can reuse authorized assets. Fittingbox advertises 195,000+ digitized frames; catalog size is not a disclosed training-set size. Fittingbox production ↗

09 / 10 · Acceptance gates

Measure product fidelity before claiming 90%+.

Suggested commercial definition: 90% of unseen products pass every agreed requirement without correction. This has not been demonstrated.

CHECK

Parts

All required visible components survive export.

CHECK

Shape

Contours, joints, thickness and dimensions meet tolerances.

CHECK

Appearance

Colour and optics match under controlled conditions.

CHECK

Automation

Include rejects and manual corrections in the pass rate.

CHECK

Capacity

Measure peak RAM, queue wait and cold/warm latency.

Compare the exported GLB at source and withheld views against independent labels or trusted geometry. A passing mesh check is not a fidelity score.

Freeze thresholds and product splits before testing. Report results by frame family; a strong average must not hide rimless or reflective-product failures.

10 / 10 · Leadership decision

Choose the route using the same product benchmark.

Preserve working try-on. Commission a bounded feasibility comparison before committing to a release scope.

Internal development

A · Build

Pretrained evidence + adjustable geometry + joint fitting + materials + QA. Retains control; specialized engineering and uncertain quality remain.

External digitization

B · Source assets

Test vendor photo-generated GLBs and manufacturer assets. Confirm API/export, licensing, turnaround and unsupported cases.

Hybrid catalog strategy

C · Combine

Reuse exact known assets; reconstruct supported new products; obtain better evidence for unresolved cases.

1 · Define

Supported frames, photos and tolerances.

2 · Compare

Current output, proposed prototype and vendor assets.

3 · Decide

Select route from fidelity, correction rate, cost and capacity.
